# Open Technology Fund

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Best direct technical route, subject to live program and eligibility.

Lead with a specific surveillance/censorship threat to civil-society users. Explain local handling, restoration-key risk, optional cloud QA and a usable deployment. Add partner demand, an independent security evaluation, language-specific tests and a maintenance budget.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- OTF funds technology that counters censorship and repressive surveillance and improves online security/privacy. [Source](https://www.opentech.fund/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/OTF-Annual-Report-FY-2022.pdf).
- IFF is an open, transparent, competitive process for technology implementation, applied research and digital security. [Source](https://www.opentech.fund/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/OTF-Annual-Report-FY-2022.pdf).
- Public site says its funds and research support individuals and organizations with technology-focused projects serving people subject to digital authoritarianism. [Source](https://www.opentech.fund/).
