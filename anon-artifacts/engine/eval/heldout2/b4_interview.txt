INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT -- "Harbour Voices" oral history project
Recorded 14 July 2021 (2021-07-14), Santos, Brazil. Transcribed by volunteer.

HANA KOBAYASHI-BRANDT (interviewer): Okay, we're recording. Say your name for the tape?

AMARA DIALLO: Amara Diallo. Although in the port everybody says "Mama D", so if you ask for Amara, nobody knows who you mean. [laughs]

HK-B: And you came from Dakar in, what, 1998?

AD: Ninety-eight, yes. Well -- ninety-seven, my papers say March 3rd, 1998, but I left in the December. I was twenty-two. I lived first at Rua das Palmeiras 88, apto 12, Sao Paulo 01310-100, with my aunt.

HK-B: And the first job?

AD: Cleaning offices. Then the pilots' station. Honestly it was the best thing, I became the only female harbour pilot in the whole of Santos, and the men never forgave me for it.

HK-B: Who helped you?

AD: Dmitri Volkov. Dmitry Volkoff on his old passport, Dima to his friends. He worked for Santos Marine Pilots Association and he vouched for me. Lucia Fernandez-Ortega, "Lucy", did my paperwork. My CPF is 987.654.321-00, I memorised it before my own birthday! You can put that, it is old.

HK-B: [crosstalk] Sorry, say that again?

AD: 987.654.321-00. CPF. Anyway.

HK-B: Can I reach you again if I need a follow-up?

AD: WhatsApp me, +55 11 91234-5678, or mail amara.diallo@harbourvoices.example.org. On Instagram I am @mama_d_pilot.

HK-B: Great. For the archive upload, the project gave me a test credential. Don't laugh, it's literally: sk_test_EXAMPLE_4fG9hJ2kL8mN. We'll swap it before launch.

AD: (laughing) Do not lose it. Do you remember what Brazil was like when the Olympics people from the International Olympic Committee visited?

HK-B: Not really -- I was in Kyoto then!

AD: Well. Tuesday, I will tell you about the storm.
