# OpenSats

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Conditional: direct ecosystem contribution and open licensing.

Provide a concise one-page case, inspectable repository/license, direct Bitcoin/Nostr utility and public education materials. A general privacy product without ecosystem benefit remains a weak route.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- Applications are evaluated against direct Bitcoin impact, free/open source, and transparency/education; public code/docs under proper open licenses are mandatory. [Source](https://opensats.org/apply).
- OpenSats accepts worldwide, nym-friendly applicants and requests concise, well-written applications; long-term support targets critical Bitcoin maintainers. [Source](https://opensats.org/apply).
- All programs are open worldwide and nym-friendly; payout processing can require tax/payment information. [Source](https://opensats.org/apply).

- Keep the proposal to one informative page; state direct Bitcoin utility, an OSI-style open license, public deliverables, and a concrete plan to share/teach work. This follows express published criteria. [Evidence](https://opensats.org/apply). Criterion-aligned recommendation; exact wording is not a funder quote.
