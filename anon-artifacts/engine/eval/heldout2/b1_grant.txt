SECTION 2. SUMMARY OF NEED

Since 2019 we have run two converted minibuses that carry books, solar lamps and a small projector to eleven villages outside the capital. Last year, according to figures compiled by UNESCO and the United Nations Children's Fund, fewer than one child in three in our district could read a short paragraph unaided at age ten. We think that can change. Our volunteers visit each village every second Tuesday during term time, and twice weekly during Reading Week.

The treasurer is the only retired customs officer in the village who also teaches Quranic recitation on Fridays, and she has agreed to countersign every payment over 500 shillings.
