# Investigative Journalism for Europe

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Reporting-led route; eligible multinational investigation required.

Open with the cross-border investigation and publication partners. Connect pseudonymization to a documented collaboration/source-protection bottleneck, risk assessment, hard costs and independent editorial judgment.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- Cross-border relevance, public relevance, newsworthiness, research/publication plan, impact, feasibility, credentials, risk assessment and need. [Source](https://investigativejournalismforeu.net/grants/investigation-support-scheme/).
- Teams in at least two eligible European countries and publication in respected platforms in at least two eligible countries. [Source](https://investigativejournalismforeu.net/grants/investigation-support-scheme/).

- Frame the tool as risk-assessed infrastructure for a named, eligible cross-border investigation and publication plan; IJ4EU also offers a secure OSS workspace after assessment. [Evidence](https://investigativejournalismforeu.net/grants/investigation-support-scheme/). Analyst inference.
