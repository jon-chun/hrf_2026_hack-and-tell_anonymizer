# ECPMF / Media Freedom Rapid Response

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

JiR residency is a different instrument from software development.

For a real relocation request, document journalism and verified threat. For the tool, seek a separate eligible partnership/training or capacity-support route; residency precedents do not support a product-development ask.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- JiR provides temporary safety and recovery for threatened media professionals. [Source](https://www.ecpmf.eu/support/journalists-in-residence/).
- Historical 2020 call emphasizes verified threat related to journalistic work and confidential application. [Source](https://www.ecpmf.eu/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Final-3-JiR-call-2020.pdf).
- Applicant must be an eligible journalist under threat, with references and language/residency conditions; check specific call. [Source](https://www.ecpmf.eu/support/journalists-in-residence/).

## Public people and roles

Roles below are bounded to their source dates. Institutional leadership is not evidence that someone selected a particular grant or prefers a personal narrative.

- Alina Toropova — JiR Programme Manager; Shelter City Leipzig coordinator; 2025 public interview; joined ECPMF in 2020. Award-specific selection role established: False. [Source](https://sheltercity.org/shelter-city-leipzig-is-opening-its-doors-to-journalists-at-risk/).

## Communication implications

- For JiR, communicate verified need for temporary relocation and professional continuity. A software-development request requires a different program, partner or procurement route. [Evidence](https://www.ecpmf.eu/support/journalists-in-residence/). Analyst inference.

## Coverage limits

Twenty publicly named alumni support cases, not 20 software grants. Gallery verifies participation but often not individual year, amount or project deliverable. Names are already publicly disclosed; no deanonymization attempted. This cohort is intentionally a program-type counterexample, not technology comparables. Gallery ordering is not award date or priority.

20 sampled records; maximum permitted 20. No record implies recovery of the original application. For projects with unnamed individual leaders, organizational recipients are reported as organizations, not invented project managers.
