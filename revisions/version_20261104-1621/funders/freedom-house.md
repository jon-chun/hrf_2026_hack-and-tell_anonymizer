# Freedom House

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Emergency/resilience support only where actual need qualifies.

Describe the verified threat, urgency, eligible short-term cost and how the intervention lets a defender/organization continue its work. Protect identities; a long software roadmap does not establish emergency eligibility.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- EAP funds short-term emergency assistance, rapid advocacy/opportunity grants, and resiliency/security support for at-risk defenders and organizations. [Source](https://freedomhouse.org/pl/node/464).
- Dignity reviews applications for eligibility and justification and independently verifies information. [Source](https://freedomhouse.org/fa/node/483).
- EAP targets HRDs, CSOs, non-traditional advocates, and survivors of severe religious persecution facing threats; listed uses include security, relocation, medical/legal, humanitarian aid and damaged-equipment replacement. [Source](https://freedomhouse.org/pl/node/464).
- Dignity targets LGBTQI+ defenders and organizations under threat, and SOAR funds urgent short-term response to threats or opportunities. [Source](https://freedomhouse.org/fa/node/483).
