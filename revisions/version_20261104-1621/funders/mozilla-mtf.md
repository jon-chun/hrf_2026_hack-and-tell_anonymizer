# Mozilla Foundation — Mozilla Technology Fund

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Conditional: match the actual current theme/program.

Name the exact open call and public-interest community. Show a reusable open artifact, transparent evaluation and community stewardship. Historical AI/environmental awards do not prove a generic privacy fellowship is available.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- MTF supports open-source technologists addressing internet-health issues, through timely themed open calls. [Source](https://github.com/MozillaFoundation/mozilla-technology-fund).
- 2024 awards were up to $50,000 plus one year of mentorship/support, for open-source AI/environmental-justice work. [Source](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/blog/open-source-AI-for-environmental-justice/).
- The 2024 call's public PDF says it could review English, French, Spanish applications, required a budget/milestones/team/community plan, and excluded sanctioned/SDN recipients for that program. [Source](https://foundation.mozilla.org/documents/443/open-multilingual-speech-fund-2025.pdf).
