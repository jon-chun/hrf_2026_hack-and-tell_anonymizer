# National Endowment for Democracy

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Plausible civic deployment route; organizational eligibility unknown.

Center a country/region and local accountability users. Link the tool to secure documentation/reporting, deliver training and maintenance, and connect activity costs to observable civic outcomes.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- NED’s application requires organization and project information, a narrative, corresponding budget, and registration documentation or explanation; it accepts local-language narratives. [Source](https://www.ned.org/apply-for-grant/en/).
- NED states it supports locally led democratic efforts, independent media, accountability, digital resilience and information freedom. [Source](https://www.ned.org/national-endowment-for-democracy-2025-annual-report/).
- NED invites applications by world region and provides region-specific proposal contacts; work must fit democratic goals abroad. [Source](https://www.ned.org/apply-for-grant/en/).

## Public people and roles

Roles below are bounded to their source dates. Institutional leadership is not evidence that someone selected a particular grant or prefers a personal narrative.

- Damon Wilson — President and CEO; 2026. Award-specific selection role established: False. [Source](https://www.ned.org/ned-launches-new-interactive-grants-dashboard/).

## Communication implications

- Connect the technical intervention to locally led democratic outcomes, a specific country/region and target users, then make the activity plan and line-item budget correspond. Explain registration status if unregistered. [Evidence](https://www.ned.org/apply-for-grant/en/). Criterion-aligned recommendation; exact wording is not a funder quote.
- For the anonymization concept, articulate how it materially protects civic actors or independent media under surveillance and how it will be adopted and maintained; NED’s public strategy prioritizes secure communications, information access, and digital resilience. [Evidence](https://www.ned.org/national-endowment-for-democracy-2025-annual-report/). Analyst inference.

## Coverage limits

NED’s 2025 public active-grant listing intentionally omits recipient identities where disclosure can create danger. The records below are distinct, named project titles with amounts but organization and project-leader fields are deliberately unavailable; they are not application texts.

20 sampled records; maximum permitted 20. No record implies recovery of the original application. For projects with unnamed individual leaders, organizational recipients are reported as organizations, not invented project managers.
