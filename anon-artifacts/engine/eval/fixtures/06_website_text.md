# About the Lantern Rights Collective

We are a small civil society group that documents human rights violations and supports people who face unfair trials. Our work is independent and funded by individual donors and public foundations.

## Our team
**Tomasz Verhoeven, Executive Director.** Tomasz has led the Collective since 2019 and previously worked in the ombudsman office in Port Halvren.

**Priya Nandakumaran, Head of Research.** Priya coordinates field work and trains new monitors. She can be reached at research@lantern-rights.example.

**Dr. Henrik Valdane, Legal Adviser.** Henrik volunteers two days a week and represents clients before the regional court.
