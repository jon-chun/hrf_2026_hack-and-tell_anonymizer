## Documented criteria and eligibility

- BDF supports individuals/projects making Bitcoin and related freedom technologies tools for human-rights defenders in challenging political/financial environments. [Source](https://hrf.org/program/financial-freedom/bitcoin-development-fund/grantees/).
- 2025 award round stated open-source development, education, mining decentralization and privacy tools for human-rights advocates under authoritarian regimes. [Source](https://hrf.org/latest/hrf-grants-800-million-satoshis-to-22-freedom-tech-projects-worldwide/).
- BDF publicly describes eligible practitioner types as developers, activists, educators, organizers, translators and UX designers. [Source](https://hrf.org/program/financial-freedom/bitcoin-development-fund/grantees/).
