Court frees activist after four months in custody

KELLMOUTH, 19 May 2025. A court in the northern district ordered the release of activist Rashid Al-Mansouri on Monday, ending four months of pre-trial detention that rights groups called unlawful.

The ruling was read by the only woman judge in the northern district, who said the prosecution had not shown any reason to keep Al-Mansouri in custody. His lawyer, Anneliese Brooke-Tanaka, told reporters outside the courthouse that her client was in good health but tired. "We are grateful, and we will keep working on the underlying case," she said.

Al-Mansouri was arrested on 20 January 2025 at his home on Chandler Row. Officials accused him of spreading false information, a charge the Lantern Rights Collective and the Council of Europe have criticised. His sister, Layla Al-Mansoori, said the family had been living in fear. "Thank you to everyone who wrote letters," she told the local radio station.

A spokesperson for the regional prosecutor, Matteo Rinaldi-Okoye, declined to comment on whether an appeal would follow. Observers expect a decision within two weeks.
