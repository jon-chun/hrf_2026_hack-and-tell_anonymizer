STATEMENT OF WITNESS (translated from Uyghur by volunteer interpreter, some words uncertain)

I am called Abdulkadir Hesen, in the papers also Abdukadir Hasan, my friends say Abdu. I was born on 12.07.1985 in the village of Qarabagh, Tashkul district. My identity number is 650102198507123456. My wife is Mehriban Yusup and my small son is Ilham.

On the evening of the 3rd of May I was coming back from the market with my neighbour Tursunjan, only one name he has, when a white van stopped near the mosque. The plate of the van was XJ-A 7K821, I remember because my son likes numbers. Two men came out. One was called by the other Sergeant Rozi. They asked about my brother Memetjan Hesen who teaches in Aq Yar.

The baker with the green door next to the old mosque, who has six fingers on his right hand, saw all this, but he is afraid to speak. I do not give his name.

They took my telephone, number 0991 555 0142, and I also had a second one, +90 532 555 01 77. They asked for my messages on Telegram, my handle is @abdu_h_1985 and the code word for the group was "olive harvest". I told them nothing.

After one night they let me go. The date was 04.05.2024, I think, the morning. I walked three hours to my cousin Gulbahar's house at 8 Yangi Mahalla Street, Aq Yar. There I wrote this paper with help from Ms Wang Lei of the charity, who gave me the contact of the United Nations special rapporteur team. She also wrote her email, w.lei@bridgehelp.example.org, and told me to use the password hint sheet only once.

I wish to say also that my bank card number is 6222 0200 1234 5678 and they asked me about it. I do not know why. The money in my account was my mother's pension, nothing else.

I make this statement freely. I ask that my name is not used where the men can read it.

Signed with thumb, Abdulkadir Hesen, in presence of the interpreter.
