1. Summary of need
Survivors of enforced disappearance in the northern district often lack safe ways to record what happened. We propose a two-year programme that trains community paralegals, provides encrypted devices and pays for medical documentation. Our partner clinic in Kellmouth, led by Dr. Kasimir Dobrev, will supervise the legal review.

2. Activities
- Train forty paralegals in trauma-aware interviewing.
- Build a secure case system. The vendor contact is Ines Calloway-Mbeki and the hosting account was created on 18 September 2024.
- Publish an anonymised annual report.

3. Risks and safeguards
All interview files are pseudonymised before they are shared. We note one particular risk: a former detainee who is now a retired magistrate has agreed to advise us, so we ask reviewers not to circulate her biography.
