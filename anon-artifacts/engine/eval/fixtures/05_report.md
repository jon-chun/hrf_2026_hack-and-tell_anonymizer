# Annual monitoring report: northern district

## Summary
Between 1 January and 31 December 2024 the Lantern Rights Collective recorded 212 alleged violations of procedural rights across the northern district. This excerpt describes method, findings and limitations. Names of witnesses have been changed in the public version, but this internal draft still holds the original details.

## Method
Field researchers Anneliese Brooke-Tanaka and Matteo Rinaldi-Okoye visited courts, police stations and detention centres. Interviews followed a standard protocol, and every witness signed a consent form. Anneliese Brooke Tanaka also reviewed court files at the registry in Kellmouth.

## Findings
- Detainees were held beyond the legal limit in 63 cases.
- Access to a lawyer was refused in 48 cases, including that of Rashid Al-Mansouri, born on 04/07/1989, who was interviewed at 31 Chandler Row, Kellmouth, and whose passport number is R2290417.
- Medical examinations were delayed in 29 cases.
- Families reported that officials asked for payments before releasing documents.

## Limitations
The sample is not random. Witnesses who feared reprisals may have declined to speak. We therefore treat the numbers as a minimum.
