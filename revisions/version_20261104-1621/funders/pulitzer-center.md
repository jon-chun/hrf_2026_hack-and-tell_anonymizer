# Pulitzer Center

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Reporting-led route with concrete data method.

Specify the underreported public-interest question, outlet commitment, data access and verified methodology. Separate privacy of source material from safely shareable code/methods. AI-accountability topic similarity alone is not product-grant eligibility.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- ML reporting: specific public-interest reporting project, transparent/verified methodology, outlet path, reproducibility/code-data plan, budget and work evidence. [Source](https://pulitzercenter.org/grants-fellowships/opportunities-journalists/machine-learning-reporting-grants).
- General reporting grants serve journalists/newsrooms with specific story/series and outlet commitment; no broad concepts, basic equipment or startup seed money. [Source](https://pulitzercenter.org/applying-reporting-grant-frequently-asked-questions).
