# European Endowment for Democracy

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Conditional: eligible geography and locally rooted democracy actors.

Lead with a named or safely pseudonymized local partner's democratic work, why ordinary support is unavailable and how the tool enables continuity/accountability. Provide references and a proportionate operational budget.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- EED supports civil society, movements, independent media and individual activists, with a focus on actors unable to access existing EU instruments; it permits unregistered groups and operational costs. [Source](https://www.democracyendowment.eu/support).
- The Executive Committee makes funding decisions after Secretariat assessment; applications are rolling and emergency requests need exceptional, justified urgency and consequences of delay. [Source](https://www.democracyendowment.eu/support).
- EED predominantly serves the European Neighbourhood, Western Balkans and Turkey; its support page lists eligible countries and allows exceptional outside-area proposals with strong involvement of core-country actors. [Source](https://www.democracyendowment.eu/support).

## Public people and roles

Roles below are bounded to their source dates. Institutional leadership is not evidence that someone selected a particular grant or prefers a personal narrative.

- Jerzy Pomianowski — Executive Director; 2026. Award-specific selection role established: False. [Source](https://democracyendowment.eu/news/eed-annual-report-2025-launched-in-european-parliament).

## Communication implications

- Show locally rooted demand, a specific democracy problem in an eligible geography, why other instruments cannot support the work, a usable budget, and a flexible continuity plan; EED explicitly funds start-ups, core operations and crisis response. [Evidence](https://www.democracyendowment.eu/support). Criterion-aligned recommendation; exact wording is not a funder quote.
- For media/security work, foreground local audience, independent information or accountability outcome, and operational resilience rather than technology novelty alone. [Evidence](https://democracyendowment.eu/annualreport2025/iii-moldova-democracy-strikes-back). Analyst inference.

## Coverage limits

EED protects many partner identities. Named examples below are a capped, public subset from EED’s own stories and reports; pages describe support but usually omit amounts and proposal text.

20 sampled records; maximum permitted 20. No record implies recovery of the original application. For projects with unnamed individual leaders, organizational recipients are reported as organizations, not invented project managers.
