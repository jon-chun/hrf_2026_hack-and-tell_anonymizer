# European Commission Creative Europe

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Consortium workstream; program/call conditions must be checked.

Develop a real transnational newsroom partnership with languages, work packages, distribution, editorial independence and shared evaluation. Check topic-specific co-financing; avoid confusing an intermediary grant with software funding.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- Transnational media collaboration and sector resilience; awards reviewed by independent experts. [Source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/second-set-eu-supported-journalism-partnerships-help-wider-news-sector).
- Distinct collaboration and intermediary/regranting tracks; preserve editorial independence. [Source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/third-generation-eu-supported-journalism-partnerships-ready-help-news-sector).
- 2025 Collaborations call requires at least three partners in three participating countries; historical call, not verified open now. [Source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/funding/call-proposals-journalism-partnerships-collaborations-2025).

## Communication implications

- Present a cross-border newsroom workflow, shared adoption and sector benefit; do not pitch an isolated software build as a ready consortium. [Evidence](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/winners-fourth-call-proposals-journalism-partnerships-announced). Analyst inference.
