# Fund for Investigative Journalism

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Standalone software route weak; needs a qualifying investigation.

Identify the new wrongdoing question, preliminary findings, sources, reporting roadmap and US English-language publication commitment. Budget the tool only where it enables eligible reporting costs; do not omit the investigation.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- New, investigative exposure of wrongdoing; concrete question, preliminary findings, reporting roadmap, publication commitment and justified reporting-expense budget. [Source](https://fij.org/grants-faq/).
- Freelancers, staff reporters and outlets; full investigations need a US outlet commitment; foreign work needs a strong US angle and US English outlet. [Source](https://fij.org/apply-for-a-grant/).

## Communication implications

- Pitch a concrete, outlet-committed investigation into a defined abuse of power; make the tool an essential reporting cost, not a standalone product. [Evidence](https://fij.org/grants-faq/). Analyst inference.
