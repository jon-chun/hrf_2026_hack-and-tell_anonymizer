# Civil Rights Defenders

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Emergency/partnership route, not established open product R&D.

Connect a documented defender threat to a practical deployment/training intervention and safety outcome; consider a separate long-term implementation partnership. Do not present general software research as acute emergency relief.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- The Emergency Fund provides rapid, targeted support for defenders in danger, including legal aid, temporary relocation, security training and enhanced digital protection. [Source](https://crd.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2025-Verksamhetsberattelse-och-arsredovisning-pa-engelska.pdf).
- CRD’s global programmes provide security, innovation, community and emergency support; its Defenders’ Database supports documenting, analysing and visualising violations. [Source](https://crd.org/global-programs/).
- Emergency Fund support is for human-rights defenders in acute danger; public materials describe rapid support rather than an open public grant-application process. [Source](https://crd.org/emergency-fund/).

## Communication implications

- Frame the tool as a deployable digital-security measure for named human-rights-defender partners facing a documented, current threat, with rapid onboarding and safety outcome; do not present generic product R&D as Emergency Fund relief. [Evidence](https://crd.org/emergency-fund/). Analyst inference.
- For longer-term partnership discussions, connect secure handling of testimonies and documents to defender safety, accountability and the Defenders’ Database use case. [Evidence](https://crd.org/global-programs/). Analyst inference.
