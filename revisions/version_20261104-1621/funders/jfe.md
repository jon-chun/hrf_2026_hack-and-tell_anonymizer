# Journalismfund Europe

Research date: 2026-11-04. Public evidence only; baseline is a technical concept, not a grant proposal.

## Program fit and targeted revision

Program-dependent: newsroom innovation or qualifying cross-border team.

Select the actual microgrant versus cross-border program first. For cross-border reporting, specify journalists/countries, European public relevance, story plan and editorial outlets. For newsroom innovation, define a bounded prototype and audience benefit.

## Documented criteria and eligibility

- Expert-led review, added value, societal relevance, newsworthiness, original method/storytelling, feasibility, team, cross-border and EU-watchdog value. [Source](https://grants.journalismfund.eu/en/european-cross-border-grants-programme-grant-details).
- European Cross-border teams need at least two professional journalists/outlets from two European countries and 80% EU-country budget. [Source](https://www.journalismfund.eu/european-cross-border-grants).

## Public people and roles

Roles below are bounded to their source dates. Institutional leadership is not evidence that someone selected a particular grant or prefers a personal narrative.

- Anonymous rotating jury — Independent assessment jury; current programme. Award-specific selection role established: True. [Source](https://grants.journalismfund.eu/en/european-cross-border-grants-programme-grant-details).

## Communication implications

- An eligible reporting team should connect the tool to a defined cross-border public-interest investigation, expert/security review and feasible publication plan. [Evidence](https://grants.journalismfund.eu/en/european-cross-border-grants-programme-grant-details). Analyst inference.
